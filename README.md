Catch a Match is my main product. My goal is to help people move from browsing profiles to finding someone they want to spend time with.

## 💗 Catch a Match — AI hackathon prototype

I also built an AI-powered web prototype under the same name for the **Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon**, exploring the **Build for Your Own World** challenge.

The prototype explores how a conversation can help people create their profiles and understand potential matches.

- **Adaptive interviews** that learn about interests, values, communication preferences, and relationship goals.
- **Public profiles and private matching records**, with cited quotes checked against the original conversation.
- **Five compatibility dimensions**, with predefined weights and a final score calculated by the backend.
- **Evidence popups** that show the statements behind the analysis.
- **Personalized first-date ideas and illustrated avatars** inspired by what users share.

**Built with** React, Vite, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, and the Meta Model API.
